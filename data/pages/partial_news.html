Global Climate Summit Reaches Historic Agreement on Emissions

World leaders gathered in Geneva yesterday to sign a landmark accord committing to a 60% reduction in carbon emissions by 2040. The deal, brokered after three days of intense negotiations, includes binding targets for the first time and a $500 billion fund for developing nations.

Critics argue the targets still fall short of what scientists say is necessary to limit warming to 1.5°C, while supporters call it the most ambitious international climate agreement in history.
